# Customer Reaching Plan — Zero/Low Budget, PKR-Aware

Built specifically around: no paid ads, PKR conversion working against cash
spend, but time + Cursor/dev skills + AI tooling working in your favor. Every
tool below is free-tier or effectively free at your current volume.

---

## 1. Core principle

You trade time and automation-building for reach, instead of trading money
for reach. Every channel here costs $0-5/month max. The "budget" you're
actually spending is your own build time in Cursor — which is the one
resource you have in surplus relative to cash.

---

## 2. Channel-by-channel plan with free tools

### A. Cold outreach (primary channel — $0)

**Manual, human-sent, as established.** Free tools that help without costing:

| Task | Free tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Finding leads | Google Maps (manual search) | No API cost if done manually in browser at low volume |
| Finding leads at scale | Google Places API | Free tier: $200/month credit from Google, covers thousands of lookups — effectively free at your volume |
| Lead tracking | Google Sheets or Airtable free tier | Airtable free tier: up to 1,000 records/base, enough for months of leads |
| Drafting personalized DMs | Claude (you already have this) | Feed it lead details, get a tailored draft in seconds — the AI does the writing labor, you do the sending |
| Scheduling send reminders | Google Calendar (free) | Block time daily for your 20-30 DM cap |

**Where AI agents genuinely help here**: Claude/Cursor can be used to *draft* every outreach message and *build* the scraper/scoring logic (per the lead-agent SOP), but should not be used to *send* messages — that stays manual per the ban-risk rule already established. The AI's job is prep, not the risky final step.

### B. Content marketing ($0, time only)

| Platform | Free tool | What to post |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Native posting, free | Before/after chatbot demos, "one gap I found in a business's booking flow this week" style posts |
| Instagram/X | Native posting, free | Short screen-recordings of your chatbot demos in action |
| Video editing for demo clips | CapCut (free tier) or your existing autonomous pipeline | You already have a working video pipeline — repurpose it for short marketing clips, this is a real use of an asset you already built |
| Thumbnails/graphics | Canva free tier | Good enough for social posts, no paid plan needed |

**Where your existing assets help directly**: your autonomous video pipeline + Kokoro TTS, originally built for a different purpose, can be repointed at making short "here's a demo I built" clips for social content — zero extra cost since the infrastructure already exists.

### C. Communities and free directories ($0)

- Reddit (r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, trade-specific subs) — free, be genuinely helpful, not spammy
- Facebook Groups for local business owners in target cities — free to join and post
- Indie Hackers — free, good for visibility among people who refer work
- Free business directories in target countries (many small-business directories have a free listing tier) — worth 30 minutes to list your agency once live

### D. Referral engine ($0)

- Every delivered client gets a direct ask: "know another business owner who'd want this?"
- Optional: build a simple referral tracker in Airtable (free tier) — who referred whom, so you remember to thank/reward them later (even a free month or small discount costs you nothing but foregone revenue on one client)

## 3. Where AI agents specifically can help (beyond just chatbots)

This is the part most people miss — AI isn't just your product, it's also your unpaid staff for the outreach process itself.

| Task | How AI helps | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| Researching a lead before DMing | Ask Claude to summarize a business's public info (services, gaps, tone) from what you paste in — faster than manual reading | Claude (already have) |
| Writing in the prospect's likely language/register | Claude can draft outreach in the target country's language/tone if you're prospecting non-English markets | Claude |
| Generating the fake-company demo content | Claude can invent realistic business names, service lists, FAQs for your test/demo builds in seconds | Claude |
| Building the scraper + scoring logic | Cursor + Claude Code writes the actual scraping/filtering scripts from the SOP spec | Cursor (already have Pro) |
| A/B testing DM phrasing | Ask Claude to generate 3-4 variations of a DM, track which gets replies, refine | Claude, tracked manually in your sheet |
| Summarizing weekly outreach performance | Paste your tracker data into Claude, ask for a short analysis of what's working | Claude |

None of this costs anything beyond what you already have (Cursor Pro, Claude access). This is the actual leverage point for someone in your position: **you can't outspend competitors, but you can out-automate the prep work so your limited daily sending time (20-30 DMs) is spent on your best-targeted, best-written messages, not random ones.**

## 4. Weekly time-budget plan (since time is your real currency)

| Day | Activity | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Scrape/research 20-30 new leads (semi-automated) | 30-45 min |
| Mon-Fri | Send 20-30 personalized DMs/day (manual, AI-drafted) | 30-45 min/day |
| Wed | One piece of content posted (LinkedIn/IG) | 20-30 min |
| Fri | Review tracker: reply rate, what's working, follow-ups due | 20 min |
| Ongoing | Respond to replies same-day when possible | as needed |

Total weekly time cost: roughly 4-6 hours. Total weekly cash cost: $0.

## 6. What this plan deliberately does NOT include

- No paid ads (Google, Meta, LinkedIn) — wrong tool before you have proof, and PKR conversion makes even small ad budgets hurt disproportionately
- No paid outreach/CRM SaaS subscriptions — free tiers of Sheets/Airtable are sufficient at this volume
- No paid lead databases — Google Places API free tier covers your actual needed volume
- No automation of the actual message-sending step — stays manual regardless of what tools exist, due to platform ban risk

---

## 7. When to revisit spending real money

Only after: 2-3 real closed clients + real testimonials + a proven reply/close
rate from manual outreach. At that point, a small, tightly targeted spend
(e.g. boosting one strong case-study post to a specific city/niche audience,
$20-30 total) becomes a reasonable experiment — not before. Proof first,
spend second.
