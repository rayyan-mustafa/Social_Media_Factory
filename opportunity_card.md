- **Ceiling** — realistic income ceiling if it works (10 = high ceiling)

## The honest read

**The agency plan wins, but only barely — and not by enough to ignore C.**
The Tudor YouTube channel is your most mature, most differentiated asset
(you've already built agents for it, it's near monetization, and it's not
competing against your own outreach-capacity limits the way client work
does). It scored close behind mainly because YouTube revenue ramps slower
and is less predictable month to month than a service agency with direct
sales control.

**The strongest real move, given the scores, isn't "pick one" — it's
sequence both without diluting either:**

1. **Keep the agency as your primary active effort** (highest score, fastest
   real cash, most within your control)
2. **Let the Tudor channel run largely on the automation you've already
   built** (policy/trend/watchdog agents) with minimal ongoing manual
   effort — it doesn't need to compete for your daily time budget since
   it's substantially automated already
3. **Do NOT start B, D, F, or G as new active efforts right now** — they
   score lower and would dilute focus away from A and C, which is exactly
   the mistake the earlier "niche down, don't scatter" principle warns
   against

**One genuine reconsideration worth having**: if the Tudor channel is
truly close to monetization and needs very little additional manual time
because of the agents already built, it may be worth 1-2 focused days to
push it over the monetization threshold before diving fully into agency
outreach — a small time investment in an asset that's already 90% built
could unlock a second income stream running in the background while the
agency ramps up. Worth deciding explicitly rather than letting it drift.

**What NOT to do**: don't chase E (micro-SaaS) or F (Upwork) based on this
scorecard — both score lowest and both pull you toward exactly the traps
already identified (competing with funded SaaS companies, or going back to
the saturated platforms you were trying to escape in the first place).
